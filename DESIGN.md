# 10 Days to 20th May — Design Documentation

## Project Overview

A soft, emotional, faith-centered mini birthday website designed as a digital devotional countdown experience.

The goal is to make the recipient feel:

* deeply loved
* seen
* protected
* appreciated
* spiritually encouraged
* emotionally comforted

This experience should feel like a mixture of:

* a devotional journal
* a digital scrapbook
* a cinematic love letter
* a quiet late-night conversation

---

# Design Direction

## Core Emotional Keywords

* Warm
* Intimate
* Dreamy
* Peaceful
* Intentional
* Soft
* Reflective
* Romantic
* Spiritual
* Comforting

---

# Visual Identity

## Aesthetic Style

The interface should feel:

* handwritten
* cinematic

* modern but emotionally warm
* elegant and minimal

Inspired by:

* pressed flowers in books
* Bible journaling
* warm coffee shop lighting
* late-night prayer journals

* soft worship visuals

# Card Design

## Card Style

* Rounded corners
* Soft shadows
* Glassmorphism blur effect
* Semi-transparent lavender background
* Minimal icon or arrow indicator
* Soft hover/tap animations
* Gentle glow effect on interaction

## Behaviour

* Card expands OR opens modal
* Smooth transition animation
* Fade-in text animation
* Background slightly darkens

# Motion & Animation

## Animation Style

* Slow fades
* Gentle floating movement
* Smooth card expansion
* Soft opacity transitions
* Minimal but emotional

Avoid:

* aggressive motion
* flashy effects
* fast transitions

## Cards
